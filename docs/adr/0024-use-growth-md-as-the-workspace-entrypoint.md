# Use GROWTH.md as the Workspace entrypoint

A portable Growth Workspace is identified by one `GROWTH.md` OKF document at its root. Its frontmatter declares `type: GrowthWorkspace` and human-readable Product context, while its structural Markdown body orients External Agents and links to important Growth Documents. The directory is the initial physical boundary; stable UUIDs, a separate manifest, registry entries, and profile mappings wait for demonstrated identity or synchronization conflicts.
